# Security policy

This edition has a deliberate paper-only boundary. It contains no exchange or
Telegram credential, live adapter, VPS configuration, private market data or
operational log. Its identifiers and prices are synthetic.

The showcase is not the complete private prototype and is not a statement of
production readiness or complete security.

Please send suspected vulnerabilities privately to mehajardian@gmail.com.
Do not publish credentials or exploit details in an issue. External
contributions are not currently accepted.
