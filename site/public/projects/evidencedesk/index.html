A concrete frozen v7 error

Case v7-a12 asked, “When did the custody become effective, including the time?” The expected source was chain_of_custody.md, page 2: “Custody became effective on 27 August 2031 at 08:30.” The recorded answer instead said “Conflicting values are supported” and cited both that page and a page 3 renewal date in 2032. The relevant passage was retrieved, but the answer failed.

Hypothesis: the answer decision confused the effective date with renewal despite a relevant citation. That is an interpretation of this case, not a measured causal fix. Check the frozen case, recorded output and analysis. Later fixes did not rerun v7.
