Static walkthrough • synthetic examples • application runs locally.

The answer must carry its evidence.

EvidenceDesk combines asynchronous document processing, page-level citations, extraction, explicit abstention, and local evaluation for synthetic supplier-operation documents. It is a release-candidate prototype, not a validated service for production, legal, medical, financial, or compliance decisions.
